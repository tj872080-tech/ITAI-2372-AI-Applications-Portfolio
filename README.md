## 💡 What I Learned

One of the most important lessons from this course is that AI is not limited to one industry or one type of problem.

AI can be used to analyze large amounts of information, identify patterns, automate repetitive tasks, support decision-making, and improve efficiency.

At the same time, developing AI systems requires more than technical performance. **Privacy, fairness, transparency, security, accountability, and human oversight** are important when AI affects people and organizations.

I also learned that different technologies can complement each other. For example, AI can provide intelligent analysis while blockchain can provide trusted and tamper-resistant records.
